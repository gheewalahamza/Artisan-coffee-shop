Know Your Coffee Beans

Coffee beans have different characteristics depending on their variety, growing conditions and processing methods. Explore two of the most popular coffee species.

Arabica

Arabica coffee is known for its smooth flavour, pleasant aroma and balanced acidity. Depending on its origin, it may have notes of chocolate, fruits, caramel or flowers.

Characteristics

  • Smooth and complex flavour
  • Often has fruity or floral notes
  • Generally lower in caffeine than Robusta
  • Commonly grown at higher elevations

Robusta

Robusta coffee is known for its strong flavour, fuller body and higher caffeine content. It often has earthy, nutty and chocolate-like notes and is commonly used in espresso blends.

Characteristics

  • Strong and bold flavour
  • Generally higher in caffeine than Arabica
  • Often has earthy or nutty notes
  • Can produce a rich espresso crema

Around the World in Every Cup

The place where coffee is grown influences its flavour, aroma and overall character. Discover some of the world's well-known coffee-growing regions.

Ethiopia

Ethiopia is widely recognised as the birthplace of Arabica coffee. Ethiopian coffees are often associated with floral aromas, fruity flavours and bright acidity.

Flavour Notes: Floral, Berry and Citrus

Colombia

Colombia is famous for its high-quality Arabica coffee grown across mountainous regions. Many Colombian coffees offer a balanced flavour, pleasant acidity and a smooth finish.

Flavour Notes: Caramel, Nuts and Red Fruits

Brazil

Brazil is one of the world's leading coffee producers. Brazilian coffees are often known for their chocolate-like flavour, nutty character and balanced sweetness.

Flavour Notes: Chocolate, Hazelnut and Caramel

India

Indian coffee is grown in regions such as Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Indian coffees can offer a variety of flavour profiles, including spicy, earthy and chocolatey notes, depending on the variety and processing.

Flavour Notes: Spice, Chocolate and Nuts

The Art of Coffee Roasting

Roasting transforms green coffee beans into aromatic brown beans and develops their flavour. Different roasting levels create different tastes, aromas and colours.

Light Roast

Lightly roasted beans generally retain more of their origin characteristics. They often have bright acidity and delicate fruity or floral flavours.

Flavour Profile: Bright, Fruity and Floral

Medium Roast

Medium-roasted beans offer a balanced combination of aroma, sweetness and body. They are a popular choice for many everyday coffee beverages.

Flavour Profile: Balanced, Sweet and Nutty

Dark Roast

Dark-roasted beans develop a deeper colour and stronger roasted flavours. They often have bold, smoky or bittersweet characteristics.

Flavour Profile: Bold, Smoky and Bittersweet

Discover Your Flavour

Chocolate

The Journey of Our Coffee

A memorable cup of coffee requires care at every stage, from growing the beans to preparing the final beverage.

01. Growing

Coffee plants grow in suitable climates, where conditions such as temperature, rainfall and soil affect bean development.

02. Harvesting

Ripe coffee cherries are harvested and prepared for further processing.

03. Processing

The coffee cherries are processed to remove the fruit and prepare the beans for drying and storage.

04. Roasting

Green coffee beans are roasted to develop their colour, aroma and characteristic flavours.

05. Brewing

The roasted beans are ground and brewed to create a delicious cup of artisan coffee.
